Community Planning Workbook

A free spreadsheet that helps your group discern where and how to live near one another: align on what matters most, compare neighborhoods, track properties, and keep your plan in one place.

What's inside

  • Community Members: each household's budget, timeline, and commitment, plus your group's shared budget window
  • Housing Priorities: every household ranks 30 characteristics; the workbook finds your non-negotiable, top priorities, and where you disagree
  • Neighborhood Comparison: score up to 5 neighborhoods, automatically weighted by what your community values most
  • Properties: track homes, lots, and land with price per home, status, and ratings
  • Community Dashboard: vision, strategy, best-fit neighborhood, roles, stakeholders, and next steps on one page
  • Opens in Excel or Google Sheets

01 / The Problem

We long for community. But modern life has pulled us apart.

Many of us deeply value our friends, yet live 20–45 minutes from one another. The ordinary practices of shared life end up needing constant planning.

Today

Scattered

Friends become appointments across town. Care requires coordination instead of presence.

Proximate

Near one another

Shared meals, childcare, and spontaneous hospitality happen naturally when people live close.

Friends become appointments across town.

Neighboring stays optional and unfamiliar.

Proximity can restore the shared rhythms of life.

02 / The Vision

What if we chose proximity on purpose?

Community becomes easier to practice when people live near each other. Proximate helps groups reconsider where they live as part of how they build community.

  • Friends living next door
  • Shared meals
  • Children growing up together
  • Everyday mutual care
  • Hospitality
  • Neighborhood investment
  • Local mission
  • Long-term rootedness
03 / What Is Proximate Community?

A proximate community organizes life around four things.

01

Shared Life

Regular rhythms of gathering and mutual support.

02

Formation

Growing as whole people and households alongside one another.

03

Mission

Seeking the flourishing of neighbors and marginalized people.

04

Rootedness

Establishing long-term roots in a particular place.

04 / Three Pathways

There's more than one way to live near your people.

Existing Neighborhood

Households buy or rent homes near one another in a neighborhood that already exists.

→

New Development

A community works with developers, builders, investors, or landowners to create a cluster of homes.

→

Shared Property

A group acquires or shares a property with multiple dwellings or another shared arrangement.

Not sure yet? That's okay. Proximate helps you explore before choosing a model.

01 / The Problem

We were made for community. But modern life has pulled us apart.

Many of us crave consistent rhythms with our people, yet live 20–45 minutes from one another. The ordinary practices of shared life end up staying out of reach as distance keeps us isolated to our individual lives.

Today

Distance

Life is scattered across town. Friendship revolves more around “catching up” than “walking with” one another.

Proximate

Nearness to one another

Community is rooted in a particular place, creating opportunity for shared life and daily rhythms.

Close friendships are declining rapidly.

1 in 2 Americans have three or fewer close friends, nearly double the share in 1990.

Neighboring is optional and unfamiliar.

1 in 4 adults under 30 don’t know a single neighbor.

Loneliness is becoming the new norm.

1 in 3 adults feel lonely at least once a week.

Proximity creates the context for life together.

Sources: Survey Center on American Life (2021) · Pew Research Center (2018) · American Psychiatric Association (2024)

02 / The Vision

What if we chose proximity on purpose?

Community becomes easier to practice when people live near each other. Proximate Communities helps groups reconsider where they live as part of how they build community.

  • Friends living next door
  • Shared meals
  • Everyday mutual care
  • Hospitality
  • Neighborhood investment
  • Local mission
  • Long-term rootedness
  • Children growing up together
03 / What Is Proximate Community?

A proximate community organizes life around four things.

01

Shared Life

Regular rhythms of gathering and mutual support.

02

Formation

Growing as whole people and households alongside one another.

03

Mission

Seeking the flourishing of neighbors and marginalized people.

04

Rootedness

Establishing long-term roots in a particular place.

04 / Three Pathways

There's more than one way to live near your people.

Existing Neighborhood

Households buy or rent homes near one another in a neighborhood that already exists.

→

New Development

A community works with developers, builders, investors, or landowners to create a cluster of homes.

→

Shared Property

A group acquires or shares a property with multiple dwellings or another shared arrangement.

Not sure yet? That's okay. Proximate Communities helps you explore before choosing a model.
